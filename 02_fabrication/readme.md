# Simple BOM

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Armature (Moving Part):**
> - 8 × N52 magnets (10 mm diameter × 10 mm axial length)
> - 80 mm carbon fibre tube (10 mm inner diameter × 12 mm outer diameter)
> - Superglue (to secure end magnets)
>
> **Stator (Stationary Part):**
> - 2 × N52 restoring magnets (12 mm diameter × 3 mm axial length)
> - 0.2 mm enameled copper wire (~2-5 m)
> - ABS or PLA filament (for 3D printed housing)
> - 3 × M3 × 20 mm bolts (to secure stator cap)
>
> **Tools:**
> - 3D printer (Voron 2.4 or similar)
> - Oscilloscope (for testing)
